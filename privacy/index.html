One Up: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

One Up is a golf scoring app for societies and golf trips, run by Matthew Simpson (contact: matt@oneupapparel.com). This policy explains what the app stores, why, and the choices you have.

What stays on your iPhone

Your own rounds, scores, clubs, course notes and profile photo are stored on your iPhone. Your location is used on your iPhone only, to show distances on the course. It is never sent to us.

What we store if you create a One Up account

An account is optional. It is needed for societies, competitions, live scoring and your Player Card. With an account we store:

  • your email address (to send your sign-in code; it is never shown to other golfers);
  • the username, display name, home club, Handicap Index and photo you choose to add to your profile;
  • the societies you belong to, and your role in them;
  • competitions you enter: your frozen competition handicap, your scores, marker checks and results;
  • names and handicaps of guests that an organiser enters for a competition.

Other members of your societies can see your profile details, entries, scores and results within those societies. Golfers outside your societies see only what you make public on your profile.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores account and society data on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails from no-reply@oneupapparel.com.

We don't sell your data, show adverts, or use third-party tracking or analytics.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Account > Delete account). This permanently removes your profile, photo, society memberships, posts, friends, and your competition entries, scores and results. Societies you set up stay with their other organisers, if they have any; otherwise they are deleted too.
  • To ask for a copy of your data or for help with anything in this policy, email matt@oneupapparel.com.

Children

One Up is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
