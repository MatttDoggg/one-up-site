One Up: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

One Up is a golf app for scoring, improving your game, and running societies and golf trips. It is run by Matthew Simpson (contact: matt@oneupapparel.com), who decides how your information is used. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who helps us process it, and your choices.

What stays on your iPhone

Your rounds, scores, the shots you log (club, where you hit from and the lie), clubs and distances, targets, course notes and imported golf history are stored on your iPhone.

Your location is used on your iPhone to show distances, to record where you hit a shot when you log one, and, only if you switch on "Help check this course's map" for a course, to keep a record of where you walk during rounds there. All of this stays on your iPhone. Your location is never sent to us.

Apple services the app uses

  • Apple Weather: to show live wind and temperature, One Up sends the position of the green you are playing to Apple's weather service. It is not linked to you or your account.
  • Apple Maps: when you search for a golf club, the words you type are sent to Apple Maps.
  • Apple Push Notification service: if you allow notifications, Apple gives your iPhone a notification address, which we store with your account so we can send you notifications.

What we store if you create a One Up account

An account is optional. It is needed for friends, societies, competitions, live scoring and your Player Card. With an account we store:

  • your email address (to send your sign-in code; it is never shown to other golfers);
  • the username, display name, home club, Handicap Index and photo you add to your profile;
  • your friends and friend requests;
  • the societies you belong to, your role in them, and what you post there (announcements, results, comments);
  • competitions you enter: your frozen competition handicap, your scores, marker checks, disputes and results;
  • names and handicaps of guests that an organiser enters for a competition;
  • your notification settings and your iPhone's notification address.

Who can see what

  • Any golfer signed in to One Up can find your username, display name and photo (by searching your username or scanning your 1UP Code), so that friends can add you. Your email is never shown.
  • Members of your societies can see your profile details, entries, competition handicaps, scores and results within those societies.
  • Your Handicap Index is shown on your profile only if you choose to show it.
  • If you send an invite from the app (Invite to One Up), the picture shows the One Up logo and your first name. It goes only where you choose to share it. The invite link carries only your 1UP Code.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores account and society data on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails from no-reply@oneupapparel.com.
  • Apple provides notifications, weather and map search, as described above.

We don't sell your data, show adverts, or use third-party tracking or analytics.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed straight away; any copy in a backup is deleted within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Account > Delete account). This permanently removes your profile, photo, friends, society memberships, posts, notifications, and your competition entries, scores and results (they no longer appear on leaderboards). Competitions and invitations you set up stay with the society. If you own a society, ownership passes to one of its organisers; if it has other members but no organiser, the app asks you to hand over ownership first; if you are its only member, it is deleted with your account.
  • Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email matt@oneupapparel.com. If you are unhappy with our reply you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

One Up is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
