Questions

Is it free?

Yes, One Up is a free beta on iPhone.

Which courses have GPS?

Alresford, Hurtmore, North Hants, Alton, Farnham and Hindhead. Scoring works at any course.

Do I need an iPhone?

Yes. The Apple Watch is optional, and there's no Android version yet.
