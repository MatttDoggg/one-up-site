Your golf companion

Yardages to the green and the hazards, your card and Stableford points as you play, coaching from your own rounds, and your society's live leaderboard and league table. On your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Get the One Up beta How to join

Free beta on iPhone. You'll need Apple's free TestFlight app first: the steps are below.

GPS DISTANCES

Know the number before you pull a club

  • Front, middle and back of the green from where you stand, with how far it is to reach and to carry the bunkers, water and out of bounds.
  • Plays Like adds in live wind, temperature and the rise or fall to the green. It switches off in competition rounds.
  • Lock your phone and the hole, your score and the distances stay on the lock screen.

Hole maps and GPS at Alresford, Hurtmore, North Hants, Alton, Farnham and Hindhead. Scoring works at any course.

SCORING

Your card, kept for you hole by hole

  • Tap your score on each hole. Stableford points or your medal total add up as you go.
  • Your playing handicap and the shots you get on each hole are worked out for you from the course's card.
  • No signal? Every score saves on your iPhone straight away, and society scores send once you're back in range.
IMPROVE YOUR GAME

See where your shots are going

  • Coaching from your own rounds: where you drop shots after 5 rounds, then club and course management advice after 10 with your shots logged.
  • Practice sessions built around your weak spots, timed and scored, for the range, the putting green or at home.
  • Your ladder rewards you for practising: points for every session and a new rung to aim for.
GOLF WITH YOUR MATES

Your society, live on every phone

  • Add friends with your 1UP code and share your Player Card.
  • Run your society or golf trip: competitions, entries and a live leaderboard everyone can follow from the course.
  • Leagues with prizes and forfeits for you and your mates: a season table of your best Stableford rounds that updates as you play.
  • Send the results and the table straight to WhatsApp.
APPLE WATCH

A rangefinder and scorecard on your wrist

  • Live front, middle and back to the green on a satellite map of the hole, counting down as you walk. Turn the Digital Crown for the distance to reach and carry each hazard.
  • Swipe once after each hole to enter your score and putts. Walk off a green without scoring and One Up asks for it, already set to par.
  • Stays on through the round with your wrist down, and puts the hole and your yardages on your watch face.

Start the round on your iPhone and the Watch picks it up. Plays Like and wind show in practice rounds only.

Questions

Is One Up free?

Yes. One Up is in a free beta on iPhone, installed through Apple's free TestFlight app.

Which courses have GPS?

Hole maps and GPS distances are ready at Alresford, Hurtmore, North Hants, Alton, Farnham and Hindhead. Scoring, handicaps and society competitions work at any course.

Does it work on Apple Watch? Is there an Android version?

Yes to Apple Watch: the Watch app comes with the iPhone app and installs by itself. One Up needs an iPhone, and there's no Android version yet.

What happens to my data?

Your rounds, shots and your location stay on your iPhone. An account is optional: you need one for friends, societies and live scoring, and other golfers never see your email. There are no adverts and no tracking. The privacy policy has the full detail.
