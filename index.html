Apple Watch

Your Watch sees every shot.

Leave your phone in the bag. Your Watch shows the yardages, feels each swing, marks the shot where you hit it and works out how far it went. Score with a swipe, and feel a tap when a shot is counted.

Improve

It knows where your shots go. And why.

Every tracked shot builds your own record: how far each club really goes, which way it misses, and what your swing was doing when it did. One Up joins the dots and tells you, in plain English.

“Your 5-iron goes right. On those shots your Watch shows your lead wrist about 7° more open at impact.”

Hole more putts.

One Up Handicap

A handicap that moves with you.

Start from your official index and One Up works out a handicap from every full round you play, the way the World Handicap System does: your best 8 of 20, a cut after an exceptional round, and caps after a bad spell. New to golf? Play three rounds and get your first.

One Up's own figure for your own rounds. Society competitions use their own handicaps.

How the numbers work

Numbers you can stand behind.

Handicaps, the World Handicap System way

Your best 8 of your last 20 differentials, a cut after an exceptional round and caps after a bad spell. Course and playing handicaps use the tee's real Course and Slope Rating, never a guess.

A marker on every card

In a society match day each card is checked by a playing partner before it counts. Any change after that is logged with who made it and why.

Courses from real data

Hole maps are drawn from open map data and Environment Agency height data, and matched to the club's own scorecard. Where a number can't be found, it is left out, not invented.

Longest drive, measured

Measured from the tee to where the ball came to rest, confirmed by someone in the group and hidden until scoring closes, so the last group out still has a chance.

Make it yours

Play in your colours.

From Clubhouse green to Links navy, pick the look that feels like your club. Scores keep their own colours in every palette, so a birdie always looks like a birdie.

Questions

Is One Up free?

Yes. GPS, scoring, your Watch, the One Up Handicap and joining societies are free. Pro and Elite add shot tracking, Strokes Gained and swing and putting analysis. Everything is free during the beta.

What is a season ticket?

Six months of Pro, Elite or Society, paid once from the day you buy it. It never renews by itself; we remind you before it ends.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. GPS, scoring and swing videos work on iPhone alone. Shot tracking, swing stats and putting stroke need an Apple Watch on your lead wrist.

Which courses have GPS?

138 courses across Surrey, Hampshire, Buckinghamshire, Oxfordshire, Kent, Berkshire, Wiltshire, Dorset, West Sussex and East Sussex, with more mapped every week. Scoring works at any course.

Is it an official handicap?

No. The One Up Handicap is worked out the way the World Handicap System does it, from your One Up rounds, but it is One Up's own figure and nothing is sent to England Golf.

Can I score for my whole group?

Yes. Choose Group when you start a round and add your playing partners by their One Up code or by name. You mark everyone on your phone, each player keeps their own card and handicap, and players with One Up get their card in their own app when the round finishes.

Which games can we play?

Stableford and medal on everyone's card, plus skins (net or gross, with carry-overs), match play and better ball, worked out as you score. Society match days add skins across the field, better ball pairs and four-ball matches from the draw.

Do my society's members have to pay?

No. Members are always free. The Society plan is for the organiser who runs it, and everything is free during the beta.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, in testing. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download. iPhone and Android golfers play in the same societies; the Apple Watch features are iPhone only.
