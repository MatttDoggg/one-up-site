Play

Scores go in on the course and land on every phone.

  • Four-ball draw with tee times, random or by handicap
  • Each card marked within the group
  • Works without signal and catches up later
  • Live leaderboard and a Lock Screen live score
  • Skins, better ball pairs and four-ball matches alongside

Final in minutes, not by Wednesday.

  • Countback on ties, the way the club does it
  • Longest drive and nearest the pin
  • Order of merit: by position, best scores or head to head
  • Society stats: wins, averages, form and records
  • A results card to share in the group chat

Questions from organisers

Do my members have to pay?

No. Members are always free. The Society plan is for the organiser, and everything is free during the beta.

We play a different course every month. Does that work?

Yes. Every match day has its own course and tees. Courses One Up has mapped come with GPS and the scorecard filled in; anywhere else, you enter par and stroke index from the scorecard once and scoring works the same.

Can guests play?

Yes. Add a guest with their handicap. They play on the day and appear on the leaderboard.

Which handicaps does it use?

Your choice for each match day: members' Handicap Index or your society's own handicaps, with an allowance and an optional limit. Playing handicaps are fixed when the match day is ready, so they can't change mid-round.

What if someone has no signal on the course?

Scores are kept on the phone and sent as soon as there is signal. Nothing is lost.

Does everyone need an iPhone?

Each card needs a marker with One Up on an iPhone, and one phone can mark a whole group. Android isn't available yet.

Who can see our society?

Only your members. Leaderboards, results and stats are private to the society.
